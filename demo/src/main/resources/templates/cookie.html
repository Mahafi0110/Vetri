Our Cookie Policy

Learn how Vetri Files uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and keep our services reliable.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve performance, and provide a smoother browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Vetri Files uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Session Management: To keep you logged in and remember your active session while you use our tools.
  • Preferences: To remember language, display choices, and recent interactions.
  • Performance & Security: To help us monitor website reliability, detect errors, and protect our services.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core website functionality such as login and form submission.
  • Functional Cookies: Help provide a more personalized experience based on your activity.
  • Analytics Cookies: Allow us to understand how visitors use the platform so we can improve it.

4. Your Choices

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the availability of some features on Vetri Files.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Any third-party tools used for analytics or support are selected carefully and used only where necessary.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
